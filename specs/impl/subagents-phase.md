## Out of scope

- **In-process read-only child runner (`REQ-IP01`, P3 CANDIDATE):** Documented in `specs/subagent/features/isolation/inprocess-candidate.md` and explicitly excluded by decision D5 (`specs/batch/batch-context.md` §1).
- **Rejected designs from `specs/subagent/rejected.md`:**
  - Foreground-to-background promotion of a live child process (`rejected.md` §1).
  - Autonomous claim / persistent team task-board swarm coordinator (`rejected.md` §2).
  - Remote or container subagent spawn targets (`rejected.md` §3).
  - Full per-tool-call interactive permission pipeline (`PreToolUse`, allow/deny rule tables, `rejected.md` §4; `REQ-P01`).
  - Graceful turn-cap wrap-up prompt injection over child stdin (`rejected.md` §5; `stdin` is `"ignore"`).
  - TUI child-session tab cycling, parent/child session-tree drill-down, or live re-bootstrapping across restart (`rejected.md` §6; `packages/tui/src` remains subagent-agnostic beyond `W-13` reusing the existing background log renderer).
  - Mid-run event-driven status pushes to the parent or child model (`rejected.md` §7).
  - Bidirectional agent-to-agent messaging (`SendMessage`, `rejected.md` §9).
- **Cross-process machine-wide `maxConcurrent` lock (`specs/subagent/features/concurrency/spec.md` §2.6.3):** Concurrency remains per-process by design.
- **Unifying the three exclusive-create file lock loops (`REQ-POL04`):** `background.ts`, `experiment-registry.ts`, and `worktree-lock.ts` keep separate acquisition loops (`specs/subagent/features/durability/spec.md` §5).
- **Subprocess startup performance tuning:** No speculative spawn-latency optimisations in this phase.
